A web app that upscales and restores images with **Real-ESRGAN**, built to run on
your own machine instead of someone else's server.

## Why self-hosted

Upscaling services ask you to upload the image. For a holiday photo that's fine; for
a scanned document, an invoice, or a photo of another person, it isn't.

Here the model runs locally:

- **Unlimited processing**, because there's no quota to pay and no request to count.
- **Works offline**, once the model is downloaded.
- The image **never leaves the machine**. Not to be processed, not for anything.

<Callout tipo="nota" titulo="Hardware detection">
  On startup it checks whether CUDA is available and picks GPU or CPU-only by itself.
  Nothing to configure: on a laptop without a dedicated GPU it still works, just
  slower.
</Callout>

## Architecture

The backend is **FastAPI**, with **Redis** as the broker and dedicated workers to
spread the load. That way one heavy request doesn't block the rest: it enters the
queue, a worker picks it up, and the UI reports progress.

Everything ships in **Docker** behind **Nginx**, so bringing it up is a
`docker compose up` rather than an afternoon of Python dependencies.

## Photos and anime don't upscale the same

They're two different models on purpose. A photo has grain, sensor noise and soft
transitions; a drawing has clean lines and flat colour areas. The photography model
smooths a drawing's linework, and the anime model turns photo grain into blotches.
Picking the right model matters more than turning up the scale factor.
